Executive Brief: MoveInSync Rating Turnaround Strategy

Prepared by Forward Deployed Engineer • October 2026
Core Problem: While MoveInSync's lifetime score reads 4.64★ (buoyed by 114k historical ratings), the 2026 run-rate score has plummeted to 3.75★, with 25.8% of incoming reviews being 1-star. Google Play's regional weighted algorithm is actively lowering public store visibility.
Top Root Causes Discovered:
  1. The "Rating Modal Hostage" Anti-Pattern: The app forces a post-ride rating popup with no skip option. Riders head straight to Google Play to leave retaliatory 1-star reviews.
  2. Post-Update Crash Loop: App updates leave Android clients in a crash/blank screen loop due to corrupted SQLite migrations, forcing users to uninstall/reinstall.
  3. OTP Blocked by Safety Popup: Standing at the curb, tapping "View OTP" launches an unskippable "Seatbelt Safety" modal while the driver honks and waits.
  4. Live Tracking & ETA Failures: Map auto-resets zoom every 30 seconds to the full route, preventing riders from tracking approaching vehicles; ETA is 3x lower than Google Maps reality.
  5. Support Black Hole: In-app tickets are closed with generic canned responses, forcing users to use Google Play as their only public escalation channel.
Engineering Action Plan:
  • Sprint 1 (Days 1-14): Add "Skip" button on rating modal; implement Google Play In-App Review API; patch SQLite migration fallback; remove seatbelt modal intercepting OTP; persist auth tokens in Android Keystore.
  • Sprint 2 (Days 15-30): Fix map camera auto-zoom; extend live vehicle tracking to 120 mins; integrate traffic matrices into ETA; add monthly roster booking grid; auto-reconcile phantom "trip not booked" pushes.
  • Sprint 3 (Days 31-60): Cap vehicle detour ratio at max 1.35x direct transit time; launch 12-hour developer response SLA workflow for Play Store reviews.
Target Outcome: Lift incoming rating from 3.75★ to 4.56★ within 90 days, eliminate >70% of 1-star reviews, and restore employee trust.
